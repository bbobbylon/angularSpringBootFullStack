TesseraApp

What TesseraApp can do

A customer and invoice management platform built around a real, enterprise-grade identity layer — not a login screen bolted onto a CRUD app.

Customers & Invoices

Track customers, generate and search invoices, and export either list to a formatted spreadsheet — or a single invoice straight to PDF for printing or emailing.

Services Catalog

Define what you sell once and attach it to any invoice — with a public browsing page prospective customers can view before they ever create an account.

Real Multi-Factor Authentication

Passkeys (WebAuthn), an authenticator app with one-time recovery codes, and phone-based verification — your choice, self-service, from the Account Security Center.

Sign In With Google, GitHub, or Microsoft

Federated login on top of your own credentials, not instead of them — link or unlink any provider from your account whenever you want.

Roles, Permissions & Organizations

A full roles × permissions matrix, an admin user directory with per-account audit history, and organization-scoped administration for multi-team accounts.

Security Monitoring

Unfamiliar sign-ins trigger an automatic step-up challenge. Admins get a live security dashboard; every user can see and revoke their own active sessions and devices.

Billing & Analytics

Revenue trends, invoice status breakdowns, and service utilisation reporting for the whole business — or scoped to your own organization.
