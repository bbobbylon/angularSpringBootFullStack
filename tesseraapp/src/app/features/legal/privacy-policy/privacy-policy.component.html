TesseraApp

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. What we collect

When you create a TesseraApp account, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored only as a salted BCrypt hash — we never store or can recover your plaintext password). If you enable SMS-based two-factor authentication, we collect the mobile phone number you provide for that purpose. If you sign in with Google, GitHub, or Microsoft, we receive your name and email address from that provider. If you register a passkey (WebAuthn), we store the public key and metadata your device's authenticator generates — never anything that could be used to recreate it. For account security we also log device and network information (browser, approximate network, timestamps) associated with sign-in events.

2. How we use it

  • To create and operate your account, and to show you your own data within the app.
  • To secure your account: detecting unusual sign-ins, enforcing multi-factor authentication, and maintaining an audit trail of security-relevant events on your account.
  • To deliver one-time verification and authentication codes — by email always, and by SMS only if you have opted in to SMS-based two-factor authentication (see §3).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Text messaging (SMS) data

If you opt in to SMS-based two-factor authentication, your phone number is used solely to deliver one-time login codes to you. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories of information sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information is not shared with any third parties, other than the messaging platform (Twilio) we use to transmit the message itself.

You may withdraw SMS consent at any time from your account's Security Center by disabling SMS-based two-factor authentication. See our Terms & Messaging Terms for opt-in, opt-out, and message-frequency details.

4. Data retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. Security audit events are retained to preserve the integrity of the audit trail. You may request account deletion by contacting us at the address below.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the account holder via the contact details on the TesseraApp GitHub repository.
