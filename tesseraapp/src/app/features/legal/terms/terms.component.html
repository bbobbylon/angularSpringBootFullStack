1. Use of the service

TesseraApp is a customer and invoice management application. By creating an account, you agree to use it only for its intended purpose and to keep your credentials confidential. You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide.

2. Account security

You may strengthen your account with an authenticator app (TOTP), a passkey, or SMS-based two-factor authentication. Enabling any of these is optional and self-service, managed entirely from your account's Security Center.

3. SMS Messaging Terms — Two-Factor Authentication

Program
TesseraApp SMS Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
Description
Sends a one-time numeric code by text message to verify your identity at sign-in, for accounts that have chosen SMS as their second authentication factor.
How you opt in
By logging into your TesseraApp account, opening the Security Center, entering your mobile number, and enabling SMS-based two-factor authentication. This is an explicit, authenticated, self-service action — your number is never collected anywhere else and is never added to this program without you doing this yourself.
Message frequency
Varies by usage — you receive exactly one message per sign-in attempt that requires SMS verification, and no messages otherwise. This is not a recurring or marketing program.
Message & data rates
Message and data rates may apply, per your mobile carrier plan.
How to opt out
Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or disable SMS-based two-factor authentication from your account's Security Center — either immediately stops all further texts to that number.
Help
Reply HELP for assistance, or see the contact details in our Privacy Policy.
Carriers
Supported by all major U.S. mobile carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
